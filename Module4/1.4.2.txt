Task 2 – Classification of Performance Variables

1. Arrival Variables

These variables describe the arrival of clients into the system. For example, A tells us how many patients arrive at the health centre. The information is updated every time a new patient arrives. It helps estimate the demand for healthcare services.

2. Service Point Variables

These variables describe the operation and efficiency of a service point. For example, B tells us the time spent serving patients and C tells us the number of patients served. These values can be used to calculate utilization (U), throughput (X) and average service time (S).

3. System-Level Variables

These variables describe the operation of the whole system from the patient's point of view. For example, Rᵢ tells us the total time an individual patient spends in the system, W represents the sum of response times, R represents the average response time and N represents the average number of patients in the system.

These variables help evaluate waiting times and the overall efficiency of the health centre.
